# Code of conduct

Bussola follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Back to the [README](README.md).

## In short

Be kind and assume good faith. Questions from newcomers are welcome, and so is disagreement about the code, as long as it is about the code.

Not accepted, here or in any space where you represent the project:

- insults, harassment, and personal or political attacks;
- sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind;
- publishing someone's private information, such as their address or their financial data, without their permission;
- any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

Write to the maintainer privately, at the address on [their GitHub profile](https://github.com/marc0todar0). Every report is read and kept confidential. A security problem goes through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that break this code, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban a participant, following the Covenant's [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
