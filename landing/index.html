Bussola
Demo GitHub

Your spending and your investments, in one app you run yourself.

Bussola tracks bank accounts, expenses and income, and the investments you hold, on a server of your own. It is multi-user and multi-wallets: one installation serves a whole household, everyone with their own account and wallets, shared with read or read-write permissions. Nothing goes to a cloud service.

Try the demo Source on GitHub

The demo runs in your browser on invented data. Nothing you type leaves the tab, and a reload starts it over.

Day to day

Accounts, categories and transactions: what you spend, what you earn and what you move between accounts. The keypad does sums, so 12.40+7.90 is one entry and not a trip to the calculator. Search, filter, and page back through years of history.

What you own

ETFs, shares, bonds, funds, crypto and deposit accounts, split across four pillars: cash, emergency fund, planned expenses and long term. A snapshot at the end of every month keeps the history. Automatic ETF and crypto prices come from an optional service that runs next to it.

On a phone, the same app

Smaller things that matter

Multi-user, multi-wallets
Everyone has their own account and as many wallets as they need. Share one with read-write permissions with your partner, or read permissions with your accountant. The database itself enforces who sees what.
Bank mode
Press H and every amount on screen blurs, for when someone is looking over your shoulder.
Passkeys
Sign in with a fingerprint or your face. The password stays as a fallback.
Your language, your currency
English, Italian, French, German, Spanish and Chinese, and a currency for each wallet.
Your data comes and goes
Import any bank's CSV. Export a whole wallet as one file, and import it into another installation.
Nothing phones home
No telemetry and no account with anyone. Prices for ETFs and crypto are the only thing it asks the internet for.

Run it yourself

Three containers and one port, on a Raspberry Pi, a NAS or any machine with Docker. About five minutes.

mkdir bussola && cd bussola
curl -LO https://github.com/marc0todar0/bussola/releases/latest/download/docker-compose.yml
curl -L -o .env https://github.com/marc0todar0/bussola/releases/latest/download/example.env
# set your username and passwords in .env
docker compose up -d

Then open http://localhost:3000. The README links the guides to HTTPS, passkeys and updates.
